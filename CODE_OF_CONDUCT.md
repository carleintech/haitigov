# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a respectful, inclusive, and professional environment for everyone
contributing to HaitiGov, regardless of identity, background, location,
language, ability, or level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with respect and patience.
- Welcome Haitian Creole and French participation alongside other languages.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Protect privacy, security, and sensitive institutional information.
- Give credit and preserve source provenance.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and learn from review.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate
misinformation, misuse of private data, and retaliation are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner or designated
maintainers. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take
other proportionate action. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical, subject to safety and legal obligations.

This policy applies in project spaces and when a person publicly represents the
project.
