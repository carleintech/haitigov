by OMRH-CRA. The catalog pages show service names and provider labels, but this
review does not independently establish legal authority, current completeness,
or permission to republish the records in HaitiGov.

## Approved scope

The repository owner approved candidate-only import on 2026-10-02. Imported
records must remain `PENDING_REVIEW`, preserve source URLs, and stay hidden from
public results until institution-owner or second-source confirmation exists.

2. the fields and languages to capture;
3. the re-verification interval and handling of retired/changed services; and
4. the operational workflow for institution-owner or second-source review.

The candidate importer is implemented, while public service pages remain
source-gated empty states until the remaining publication decisions are made.
